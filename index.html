Complete search criteria

Timing & money

  • Sell Fossveien 4 around April / May 2027; buy after the sale.
  • Owner-only baseline: roughly 11m asking; use 12.5m total as the reconnaissance ceiling.
  • A legal rental unit likely to produce about 15,000 kr/month adds 2m to the working ceiling.
  • Any allowance above 2m needs bank confirmation and a vacancy, maintenance and tax case.

Location

  • Search Greater Stavanger broadly. Location is a preference and score — never an automatic rejection.
  • Eiganes, Stokka and Våland remain benchmarks, not a postcode fence.
  • Any neighbourhood works when family function, economics and a practical Forus commute compensate.

Family function — must survive scrutiny

  • 4+ bedrooms, or a credible and legal route to four.
  • 2+ bathrooms.
  • Two proper living zones.
  • Real private outdoor space for children — not brochure shrubbery or a disputed shared lawn.
  • Space for a rack and treadmill without deleting a bedroom, rental case or normal family life.

Preference & risk

  • A legal, rentable unit is a major positive when market rent is credible; record expected monthly rent.
  • Detached house preferred; a full duplex works when control, layout and garden rights are clear.
  • Garage / practical parking is valuable, but not allowed to rescue a weak family plan.
  • Unapproved rooms, moisture, old wet rooms, conservation and major renovation reduce the score.
  • Syrinveien 27 remains the type specimen: volume, plot and flexible zones — not a live listing.
